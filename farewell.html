A note from Arun.

When I look back at my time at TCS, I don't think first about the queries resolved, trackers updated, specifications documented, or RCAs completed. I think about the people.

This journey took me from learning the process and understanding Apple Maps workflows to taking on responsibilities as an SME, Spec & RCA analyst and Spec Team POC. Every step pushed me to learn, take ownership, and grow beyond my comfort zone.

There were days when a simple query became a full investigation, a spectrum issue became an RCA, and a “quick meeting” somehow consumed half the day. There were challenging deadlines, corrections, debates, and moments when we wondered whether a specification was written for humans or for people with extraordinary patience.

But through all of it, there were people who made things easier. People who shared knowledge, challenged ideas, supported decisions, stepped up when needed, and made sure nobody had to figure everything out alone.

To my managers, thank you for the trust and opportunities. To my fellow POCs, thank you for the debates, clarifications and shared problem-solving. To my team, thank you for making leadership a learning experience. And to my close friends, thank you for making work feel like a place where I belonged.

I may forget a few ticket IDs, specification clauses, and definitely some tracker entries. But I won't forget the people behind them.

Thank you for being part of my story.

Arun (Alu)
Formerly available for urgent clarifications. Now available for chai and catch-ups.
